# SeedShield — small volunteer pilot (do this only with real people who agree)

Nothing in this file has been run. Do not claim results until you have them.

## Purpose

Find out whether men can finish the steps without help, and whether the wording is clear and discreet. This is a usability and comprehension check, not a clinical study.

## Who

- 8 to 10 adult men (18+), friends or colleagues who volunteer.
- Not patients, not recruited through a clinic, nobody pressured.
- Each person uses their own phone and may stop at any time.

## Before you start (read aloud, get a yes)

"This is a prototype health screening tool. It is not a diagnosis and not medical advice. I am not collecting your name or health data. I will ask how the steps felt. You can skip any question or stop whenever you like. Do you agree?"

Tell them to use made-up lab numbers if they prefer not to enter real ones.

## Tasks (10 minutes)

1. Open the live link, pass the age gate, turn on the privacy switches you are comfortable with.
2. Enter a lab value and answer the weekly questions (made-up values are fine).
3. Say out loud why the app gave its next step. Can they find "Why this appeared"?
4. Create a video link, download the calendar file, add it to their calendar.
5. Read the summary and say what they would be happy to share.
6. Turn on Discreet mode and switch to Arabic if they prefer it.

## What to record (no names)

| Measure | How |
| --- | --- |
| Completed all 6 tasks without help | yes / no, per person |
| Time to finish | minutes |
| "Screening, not diagnosis" understood | ask: "Did the app tell you that you have a condition?" (correct answer: no) |
| Comfort with privacy, 1 to 5 | one question |
| Clarity of next step, 1 to 5 | one question |
| Discreet wording acceptable | yes / no, plus any word that felt wrong |
| Anything confusing | free text, one line |

## How to report it honestly

State the number of people, that they were volunteers, that data were made up or self-entered, and that this is a usability check only. Report the problems you found and what you changed. Do not call it a clinical pilot or a validation.

## Stop rules

Stop and fix before continuing if anyone believes the app diagnosed them, anyone is upset by wording, or the summary exposes something they did not expect.
