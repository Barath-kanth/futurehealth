# SeedShield rules on real measurements: NHANES 2015–2016

> Generated by `npm run validate:nhanes`. Do not edit by hand.
> **Scope:** implementation and safety checks of the triage rules on real people's measurements.
> **Not** a clinical accuracy study: NHANES has no semen, fertility, or diagnosis labels, so no sensitivity or specificity for infertility is claimed.

## Data

- **Source:** US CDC, National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES), 2015–2016 cycle. Public-domain US government data.
- **Files:** DEMO_I, BMX_I, GLU_I, TST_I, SLQ_I (https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Nhanes/continuousnhanes/default.aspx?BeginYear=2015).
- **Sample:** 1726 men aged 18–54. BMI measured for 1639; fasting glucose for 723 (fasting subsample only); total testosterone for 1547.
- **Unmeasured values are left empty, not invented.** Lifestyle and environment inputs (heat, stress, shift work) are not in NHANES and are held at neutral defaults, so this exercises the metabolic and hormone rules only.
- **Unweighted.** This is a sample, not a nationally representative estimate.

It does **not** show clinical accuracy. The glucose flag fires at ≥ 110 (WHO), so it deliberately misses the ADA 100–109 band; the ADA row quantifies that choice.

## 4. Findings to act on

- **A single low testosterone value routes straight to the specialist-review gate.** 353 of 1547 men with a measured value (22.8%) are below 300 ng/dL, and 353 of 1726 men overall (20.5%) land in the andrology/urology pathway from that one value. The AUA testosterone-deficiency guideline (2018) advises confirming a low total testosterone with a second early-morning measurement before acting, and NHANES blood draws happen in morning, afternoon, and evening sessions, so some low values here may be timing effects. Among the 573 men with BMI ≥ 30, 215 reach the specialist gate, consistent with the known association between obesity and lower total testosterone. Candidate refinement for clinician review: treat a single low value as "see a primary doctor and repeat the test" and reserve the specialist gate for a confirmed low value or another cue.
- **Glucose band inconsistency.** The Ranges page lists 70–99 mg/dL as acceptable, while the engine flags only from 110. Values of 100–109 are neither "acceptable" nor flagged. This is a WHO vs ADA difference and should be stated, or the two aligned.
- **What this cannot show.** Semen rules, heat and shift effects, the self-report path, and referral completion are not covered by this dataset.

## 5. Limits

- US sample, 2015–2016; not Gulf-specific.
- Testosterone was measured by NHANES at varying times of day; the rule assumes a morning draw.
- Fasting glucose covers only the fasting subsample (n = 723).
- A rule that agrees with its own reference threshold is a correctness check, not evidence of clinical benefit.

## Reproduce

```
mkdir -p data/nhanes/raw && cd data/nhanes/raw
for f in DEMO_I BMX_I GLU_I TST_I SLQ_I; do curl -O https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2015/DataFiles/$f.xpt; done
cd ../../.. && python3 scripts/nhanes/prepare.py && npm run validate:nhanes
```
